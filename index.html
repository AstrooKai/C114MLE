About Me

Who I am, what I study, and the stack I build with.

Steven Kyle P. Mangubat

BSIT • University of Cabuyao
  • 2nd Year BSIT Student
  • Cabuyao, Laguna, Philippines
  • Admin Officer & Developer, LagunaWISE
  • Meteorology, Geospatial & Earth/Space focus

I develop the technical backbone for LagunaWISE, a youth-led science organization that translates weather, environmental, and space science into content the public in the Province of Laguna can actually use, from typhoon updates to disaster-preparedness explainers. Alongside that, I'm finishing my BSIT with a growing interest in web development, data visualization and Philippine disaster science.
